Our story

Built by people who care for seniors every day.

HomeTogether Hire didn't come from a tech company. It came from a home care agency.

HomeTogether Hire is made by Caring Companions, a family-owned in-home senior care agency in Springfield, Missouri, since 2017. Every day, our caregivers help older adults live safely and comfortably at home.

We also talk with many families who decide to hire a caregiver on their own. That can be a good choice. But it gets overwhelming fast: applicants scattered across texts and Facebook, no idea which forms you need, and an offer letter written from scratch, or skipped.

We would rather you do it well than do it with us. So we built HomeTogether Hire to give families the same tools an agency uses: one job link for every applicant, an organized place to compare them, and hiring paperwork that is ready to sign.

What we believe

Hiring privately should be done well. Clear agreements, fair pay and the right paperwork protect your family and the caregiver.

Safety needs more than one step. A background check matters, and so do a good interview and real references. Our guides walk you through all three.

Real people stand behind it. When you email us, someone from our team answers.
