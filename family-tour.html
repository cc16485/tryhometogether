The old way

Hiring help shouldn't take over your life

Right now, finding a caregiver means posting the same plea in a dozen places, then trying to hold it all in your head:

Facebook groupsCare.com & hiring sitesThe church bulletinSchool & parent groupsNextdoorWord of mouth

Then you check every one of them, every day. You hand your personal cell number to strangers. Calls and texts come at all hours, and a week later you can't remember who Karen was, or which one had the good references. It becomes a second job, on top of everything else you're carrying.

The HomeTogether way

One organized place, like a filing cabinet

Everything about your search lives in one dashboard, sorted into simple tabs. No more scattered channels, no more handing out your number. Just open the drawer you need.

The Family team tab · everyone on the same page

Bring the whole family in, with the right access

Caring for someone is a team effort. Invite siblings, a spouse, even a niece or nephew, and give each person exactly the access that fits their role. Then decide together who's doing what, so nothing slips and no one steps on toes.

The Documents tab · sign & store, safely

Every document, signed and in one place

Made a work agreement or a consent form? Send it to your caregiver to sign online, it lands in their portal to e-sign and comes back completed. Prefer to print and sign in person? Upload the signed copy instead. Either way it's stored securely, where the whole family can see it.
