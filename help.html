Help & contact

Have a question? Email support@tryhometogether.com and a real person on our team will answer.

Is posting a job free?

Yes. Posting a job, sharing your link and seeing who applied is free. A $25/month membership adds applicant contact details, the organizer, messaging, documents and e-sign, and the caregiver directory. Cancel anytime.

Does it work where I live?

Yes, anywhere in the US. Your job link brings applicants to you wherever you live. Our caregiver directory starts near Springfield, Missouri, and grows as our team approves caregivers in new areas.

Are caregivers background-checked?

Caregivers in our directory are interviewed and background-checked by our team before their profile goes live. People who apply through your own job link have not been checked by us, so run a background check and call references before you hire. Our guides walk you through it.

Who employs the caregiver?

You do. Families hire caregivers directly, and HomeTogether Hire is not the employer. Our guides explain what a household employer needs to set up, like pay, taxes and a written agreement.

Is HomeTogether Hire part of Caring Companions?

Yes. It is made by Caring Companions, a family-owned in-home senior care agency in Springfield, Missouri, since 2017. If you live near Springfield and would rather have an agency handle everything, Caring Companions can help: mo-care.com or (417) 234-8494.

I am a caregiver. How do I join?

Create a free account on our caregiver page, fill in your profile and record your short video interview. Our team reviews every profile before it goes live. Caregivers never pay a fee.
