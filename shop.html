Good to know

Common questions.

How much does it cost?
HomeTogether is $99 per month, which includes the device, the family app, and support. Shipping is free, you can cancel anytime, and it's backed by a 30-day money-back guarantee. Optional extras like cellular internet or caregiver visits are available and priced separately.
Does my parent need a smartphone or any tech skills?
No. That's the whole point. Everything happens on the TV they already use. Calls can answer on their own, so there's nothing for them to unlock, tap, or hold.
Will it work with their TV?
It works with almost any TV that has an HDMI port. Their cable box keeps working right alongside it. You can check your TV in 30 seconds if you're unsure.
Is there a camera watching them all the time?
No. The camera has a privacy shutter that physically covers the lens, and it only opens during a call the family is part of. Safety monitoring uses non-intrusive motion and environment sensing, not recording.
What's your return policy?
Try it for 30 days. If it's not right, we refund you and send a prepaid label to mail the device back. The device stays HomeTogether property, so returning it when you're done is expected, and easy.
Can family help from far away?
Yes. Family manage contacts, reminders, and calls from a simple app on their own phones, from anywhere in the country.
Is it good for someone with dementia?
Many families use HomeTogether with a parent who has memory changes, precisely because there's nothing to learn. A familiar face on the TV, with no buttons to manage, tends to work when phones and tablets don't. Our dementia connection guide has more.
