Before you make an offer

A few things to confirm so the number you offer holds up:

  • Exact schedule and total weekly hours
  • Who covers mileage or errands driving
  • Overtime expectations over 40 hours
  • Paid time off or holidays, if any
  • Whether you'll run payroll or use a service
  • How and how often they'll be paid
